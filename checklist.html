Insider guidance

Concierge tips & tricks

Hard-won advice to make every step smoother.

📦 Boxing strategyColor-code bins by room. Gallon Ziplocs for small items. Photograph electronics wiring before unplugging. Heavy items in small boxes, light items in big boxes.
👀 Movers dayBe present the entire time. Mark pre-existing damage BEFORE movers note condition. Don't let them pack trash or hazmat. Keep a cooler of snacks.
🚗 Car shipmentOnly 1/4 tank gas. Remove all personal items, EZ-Pass, garage openers. Document exterior with dated photos. Note mileage. Keep condition report copy.
💰 Selling BMW X5Cars sell 10-20% above mainland KBB in Hawaii. Price slightly above KBB and negotiate. FB Marketplace + military swap pages get the most eyes.
🐈 Cat travelHealth cert within 10 days of travel. Airline-approved carrier — let cat get used to it weeks ahead. Book airline pet spot early.
📝 DLA & TLESubmit advance ASAP. Keep all receipts for lodging, meals, incidentals. TLE covers up to 14 days of temporary lodging.
🧽 House cleaningBook professional deep clean after move-out ($200-400). Focus on appliances, bathrooms, baseboards, windows. Photograph for deposit protection.
🪖 In-processingBring 10+ copies of orders, marriage & birth certs, POA. Takes ~1 week. Housing, DEERS, Tricare, finance — all first week.
