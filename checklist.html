Insider guidance

Concierge tips & tricks

Hard-won advice to make every step smoother.

📦 Boxing strategyColor-code bins by room. Gallon Ziplocs for small items (screws, remotes, chargers). Photograph electronics wiring before unplugging. Heavy items → small boxes, light items → big boxes.
👀 Movers dayBe present the entire time. Mark pre-existing furniture damage BEFORE movers note condition. Don't let them pack trash, cleaning supplies, or hazmat. Keep a cooler of snacks for the family.
🚗 Car shipment (Ford Focus)Only ¼ tank of gas max. Remove all personal items, EZ-Pass, garage openers. Document exterior with dated photos/video. Note mileage. Keep a copy of the condition report.
💰 Selling the BMW X5Cars sell 10–20% above mainland KBB in Hawaii thanks to shipping costs. Price slightly above KBB and negotiate. FB Marketplace + military swap pages get the most eyes.
🐈 Cat travelHealth certificate within 10 days of travel. Airline-approved carrier — let your cat get used to it weeks ahead. Bring a familiar blanket. Confirm airline pet policy and book the pet spot early.
📝 DLASubmit advance ASAP — you're authorized DLA with dependents. Keep all receipts for lodging, meals, and incidentals. TLE covers up to 14 days of lodging.
🧽 House cleaningBook a professional deep clean after move-out ($200–400). Focus on appliances, bathrooms, baseboards, windows, garage floor. Photograph after cleaning for deposit protection.
🪖 In-processing Fort StewartBring 10+ copies of orders, marriage & birth certificates, POA. Process takes ~1 week. Housing, DEERS, Tricare, finance — all in the first week.
