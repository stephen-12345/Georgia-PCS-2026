Tips & Tricks
Boxing Strategy: Color-code bins by room. Use gallon Ziploc bags for small items (screws, remotes, chargers). Take photos of electronics wiring before unplugging. Put heavy items in small boxes, light items in big boxes.
Movers Day: Be present the entire time. Mark any pre-existing damage on furniture BEFORE movers note condition. Don't let them pack trash, cleaning supplies, or hazmat. Keep a cooler with snacks/drinks for the family.
Car Shipment (Ford Focus): Only 1/4 tank of gas max. Remove all personal items, EZ-Pass, garage openers. Document exterior with dated photos/video. Note mileage. Keep a copy of the vehicle condition report.
Selling BMW X5 in Hawaii: Cars sell for 10-20% above mainland KBB in Hawaii due to shipping costs. Price slightly above KBB and negotiate. Facebook Marketplace + military swap pages get the most eyes.
Cat Travel: Get health certificate within 10 days of travel. Airline-approved carrier -- let cat get used to it weeks before. Bring familiar blanket. Check airline pet policy and book pet spot early.
DLA: Submit advance ASAP -- you're authorized DLA with dependents. Keep all receipts for temporary lodging, meals, and incidentals. TLE covers up to 14 days of lodging.
House Cleaning: Schedule a professional deep clean after move-out ($200-400 for full house). Focus on: inside appliances, bathrooms, baseboards, windows, garage floor. Take photos after cleaning for deposit protection.
In-Processing Fort Stewart: Bring 10+ copies of orders, marriage certificate, birth certificates, power of attorney. Process takes ~1 week. Housing office, DEERS, Tricare, finance -- all in first week.
